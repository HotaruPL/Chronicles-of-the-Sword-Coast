Better Immersion for Trademeet v1.1
===================================

Standalone EET component. This is NOT part of Better Immersion for Athkatla.
All new resources use QC-prefixed resrefs.

Adds ambient inhabitants to AR2000 and AR2010, including BG2 companion
interjections, a night-only spectre, and the Trademeet Haunting quest involving
Hodrig and his acolytes.

Quest rewards:
- Trademeet Haunting: +1 Reputation and 13,000 party XP on completion.

Final fixes:
- Hodrig no longer carries Slythe's Sarevok letter (SCRL2K) or Ducal Palace
  invitation (MISC78).
- Hodrig's acolytes stay neutral until Hodrig orders the attack, then turn
  hostile and begin the fight from invisibility.

The spectre appears only at night. Hodrig uses SLYTHE as a chassis but carries
DAGG04 instead of The Shadow's Blade.
