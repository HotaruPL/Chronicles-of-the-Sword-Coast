Authors: Mapek & Hotaru

Welcome!

We are thrilled to release our first mod: 'Chronicles of the Sword Coast' for Enhanced Edition Trilogy (EET).

This mod is the fruit of labor by myself and Hotaru. It is highly modular, featuring **48 independent components** that can be installed in any combination.

Have you ever wondered about the mystery of the Ice Island that Shandalar sends you to? Have you ever pondered what it would be like to encounter Astarion or Randalla Brasshorn within the classic games? Have you ever craved more Forgotten Realms-oriented cities with greater diversity among commoners and peasants? Chronicles of the Sword Coast is a component-based quest mod that adds numerous small quests to the game, alongside miscellaneous components introducing new mechanics, items, and interjections from BioWare and Beamdog characters. We specifically recommend keeping Viconia in your party, as she features the largest amount of content and additional quests.


The project started a year ago with Hotaru working alone. What was meant to be a small project with just a couple of quests quickly evolved into something much more complex. Together, we developed and merged many components into this single modification. Each quest is relatively small, ranging from simple "fetch quests" to new locations.

**Important Note on Visuals:** New areas were created using Paint and then enhanced via AI. As a result, visual quality may vary in terms of sharpness, and certain engine limitations apply (e.g., static fire animations in our custom areas). These are technical quirks we currently cannot change. Every component that adds a new location is detailed below, so if you prefer to avoid these visual limitations, you can simply skip those specific components during installation.

Requirements & Installation Order

Enhanced Edition Trilogy (EET): Required.

BP-BGT-Worldmap: Not strictly required if you aren't playing with other map-expanding mods. However, if you do use it, BP-BGT-Worldmap must be installed AFTER this mod (and after any other mod that adds new areas).
Worldmap Patch: We have included a dedicated Worldmap patch as the very last component of this mod. You can install it alongside any other desired component.

Since this is an additive WeiDU mod, it can technically be installed at any point, but we highly recommend placing it alongside other BG1 quest mods, ideally after the BG1 NPC Project.

Tested Compatibility & Load Order

Chronicles of the Sword Coast is compatible with the vast majority of WeiDU mods, including those that modify the second level of the Ice Island (such as Unfinished Business or Mysteries of the Sword Coast).

We officially tested the mod using the following install order:
1. Dark Horizons
2. Mini Quests and Encounters
3. Shades of the Sword Coast
4. Balduran's Seatower
5. BG1 NPC Project
6. Chronicles of the Sword Coast (Installed here during testing)
7. Candlekeep Revisited
8. Gorgon's Eye
9. Made in Heaven: Quest Pack
10. The Calling
11. Verr'Sza NPC (by LavaDelVortel)
12. BP-BGT-Worldmap

Known Issues & Important Notices

If you install "Better Immersion for Beregost", Neera may take longer to spawn (sometimes up to 2 minutes). If she doesn't appear right away, explore the town a bit and return to her spawn location. She should show up eventually.
If you install "Better Immersion for Baldur's Gate and Better Immersion for Beregost" installer may say it is installed with warnings. This does not cause any problems, but is strictly related to certain NPC's contained within each component.

---

Bug Reporting & Feedback

We cannot test every single WeiDU mod in existence. If you encounter any bugs or incompatibilities, please report them to us. In the event of a game crash (especially in new areas), please attach your crash dump file. This will massively help us narrow down and fix the issue.

BETTER IMMERSION SAGA (by Hotaru & Mapek)

      - Better Immersion offers additions to Nashkel, Beregost, Friendly Arm Inn, Carnival and Baldur's Gate - each in separate components. Forgotten Realms universe has always described Sword Coast as cosmopolitan environment. These components expand populated cities and villages with more races that could live there. You can meet more Halflings, Dwarves, Gnomes, Elves, Hobgoblins, half orcs or even Kobolds. Each citizen have a unique dialogue, some include interjections with joinable companions. It also adds references, characters from Baldur's Gate 3, Dark Alliance or wider Forgotten Realms lore. Components also unlock new locations such as Purple Wyrm Inn, Orphanage of Faith or Torm's Hand - new village on the World Map, and new quests, some of which can be accomplished only by having certain companions in the party. Each of new areas is a custom made area, not a recycled one. They include:
      
Torm's Hand (new village on the world map - custom area)

Red Fang Marauders Wilds (does not use world map - custom area)

New sewer section in Baldur's Gate (custom area)

Purple Wyrm Inn in Baldur's Gate (custom area)

Orphanage 'Haven's Hope' within Baldur's Gate (custom area)

HOW TO UNBLOCK ON THE WORLD MAP: 
- Torm's Hand - Speak to Randalla Brasshorn at Purple Wyrm Inn

- Red Fang Marauders Wilds - Speak to Kaeira at Purple Wyrm Inn

- New sewer section - Southern part of the eastern sewers

- Purple Wyrm Inn - Building to the left of Elfsong tavern in eastern Baldur's Gate

- Orphanage - Opposite to Oberan's Estate in Central Baldur's Gate

BAG OF HOLDING IN IRENICUS DUNGEON (by Mapek)

BETTER IMMERSION FOR CITIES IN BG2 (by Hotaru & Mapek)

      - These components offers additions to Athkatla and Trademeet. Forgotten Realms universe has always described Sword Coast as cosmopolitan environment. These components expand populated cities and villages with more races that could live there. You can meet more Halflings, Dwarves, Gnomes, Elves, Hobgoblins, half orcs or even Kobolds. Each citizen have a unique dialogue, some include interjections with joinable companions. Few mini quests are also included.

ADDITIONS TO DE'ARNISE KEEP (by Mapek)

      - Adds a few more enemies and encounters to De'Arnise Keep.

NOTE: This component is very small, but decisive. No more will be added at any point.

OLD FRIEND IN SARADUSH (by Mapek)

      - Meet a totally unexpected friendly pal from the past, who appears to be more than he ever led on. This component is not a quest, but a simple dialogue in Saradush Inn that expands one of the most mysterious characters in the series.

REPUTATION ADJUSTER (by Mapek)

      - This tweak adds a physical ability to the game to unlawfully change your reputation. Component is treated as a cheat, so if you want to play the game fairly without making exceptions, skip the component. It adds three characters:
     Mervin in Temple of Wisdom in Friendly Arm Inn
     
     Flora in Coalition Camp
     
     Sebastien in Temple of Helm in Athkatla

Talking to them will enable you to pick a dialogue option that will either decrease or increase the reputation of the party as you see fit, with no payment.

NOTE: Changing reputation can also affect shop prices, guards, and reputation-dependent quests. It won't automatically bring back companions who have already left.

FOOD AND MISCELLANIOUS MERCHANTS (by Hotaru)

      - This component adds food and miscellaneous items vendors. Food can be used to heal small amounts of health, whereas other items such as Honey or Grain can be sold to specific farmers for profit.

POTIONS OF LIFE SAVING (by Hotaru)

      - Introduces supreme potions of healing that heal 80 HP. These are very rare potions, and only a few exemplars exist in the whole trilogy. If you find one, use it with caution.

NOTE: Installing Eastern Carnival component adds one potion of life saving to new carnival area without needing to have the full potion component.
